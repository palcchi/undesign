# Audit UI Undesign #003: craft shell editor

## Scope and coverage

Scope: the shared Rust/egui editor shell, inspected at the current desktop preview (1440 × 900) and tablet preview (720 × 900). The owner has deferred mobile redesign. The current web and native desktop builds share this UI crate. Project references found: `AGENTS.md`, `undesign-ui-spec.md`, `crates/ui-egui/src/theme.rs`, and the repository Antislop pointer/skills.

The owner clarified that the earlier placement wins: keep Agent/Chat/Comment in the left rail and keep Pages | Layers | Assets as tabs. The latest request to remove the rail or combine Pages and Layers is not included. No source files were changed during this audit.

Figma’s public documentation describes a left navigation rail beside Pages/Layers and a bottom toolbar. Its UI3 article says Figma returned to docked side panels after users reported floating panels cramped the canvas and slowed work. The owner’s chosen floating toolbar and panel-edge treatment are followed as Undesign’s direction; this report does not propose copying Figma assets.

## Findings

| Severity | Domain | Location | Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MEDIUM | UI | `crates/ui-egui/src/toolbar.rs:151-189, 295-326, 508-530` | The pill is centered at a fixed canvas position. Its drag response changes horizontal overflow scroll, not the pill’s position. Tool groups have no separating dividers, the mode switcher starts 8 px after the tools, and the active mode uses a panel fill/border rather than a solid dark selection. | Make the pill a separate, draggable floating window with a grab area and persistent offset; keep it 16 px above the viewport edge. Add thin dividers between the requested tool groups, leave at least 24 px before the segmented modes, and use a solid dark active segment with white text. Retain the current tool actions and keyboard access. | A floating-looking but immovable pill does not meet the requested window behavior; group boundaries and mode selection also read less clearly than specified. |
| MEDIUM | UI | `crates/ui-egui/src/lib.rs:1845-1852, 1891-1920` | Left and right panel cards share a 1 px border, 8 px outer margin, and a shadow with offset `[0, 4]`; there is no distinct horizontal edge lift. | Keep the hairline borders and existing theme tokens, add a subtle horizontal edge shadow on the canvas-facing sides, and make content sections use a consistent 16 px inset. | The requested panels should separate from the canvas without relying on a stronger generic card shadow; consistent section padding improves scan rhythm. |
| MEDIUM | UI | `crates/ui-egui/src/right.rs:168-200`; `crates/ui-egui/src/toolbar.rs:320`; `crates/ui-egui/src/layers.rs:281` | Right-tab labels are 9.5 px; mode and left-tab labels are 10.5 px. All tabs share the same semibold weight and the right-tab padding is 10 px. The active right tab already has a 2 px accent underline and inactive tabs use the secondary text token. | Set a shared 11–13 px panel-label scale, use 12 px horizontal padding for the right tabs, make the active tab visibly bolder, and preserve the accent underline and medium-gray inactive state. | Current labels fall below the requested control scale and are too tightly packed, though the underline and inactive color are already present. |
| MEDIUM | UI | `crates/ui-egui/src/right.rs:513-524`; `crates/ui-egui/src/widgets.rs:935-942, 960-992` | The page-background swatch is 16 × 16 px. It already has a 1 px border. The hex field already has a 1 px control border and 8 px text inset. | Increase the swatch to at least 20 × 20 px; retain the existing visible field border and padding. | The swatch is undersized relative to the requested target, while the input already meets the requested boundary/padding treatment. |
| MEDIUM | UI | `crates/ui-egui/src/layers.rs:1001-1007` | The empty-file prompt appears inside the Layers list at 11 px, not in the center of the canvas. | Move the empty-state prompt to the center of the canvas, use 14–16 px text, and place a small existing monochrome frame/shape glyph above it. Keep the Layers panel empty rather than duplicating the prompt there. | The canvas is the work area; putting its first-action guidance in a side list makes the empty workspace harder to discover. |

The page and layer row heights are already 32 px (`layers.rs:16`, `widgets.rs:13`), within the requested 32–36 px range; preserve them unless the visual pass shows a concrete spacing problem. The existing Figma-like rail stays because that is the owner’s latest placement choice.

## Verification

Passed: inspected the 1440 × 900 and 720 × 900 local screenshots; read the corresponding current Rust implementations and shared widget/theme references. Verified that the toolbar shadow and panel border exist, that the hex field already has a visible border and padding, and that layer rows are 32 px.

Not verified: dark-theme screenshot, hover/focus/pressed interaction sweep, keyboard drag behavior, rendered contrast ratios, and a live Figma session. This is a source-and-preview audit, not a full interaction or accessibility audit.

**Block** — five MEDIUM craft findings remain for this requested pass. No edits have been made. The mobile redesign remains deferred.
